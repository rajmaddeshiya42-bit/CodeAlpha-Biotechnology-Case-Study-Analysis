# 🧬 Case Study Analysis of Moderna's mRNA Technology

![Cover](Cover.png)

## 📖 Overview

This project presents a comprehensive case study on Moderna Inc. and its revolutionary mRNA technology. The presentation explores the company's biotechnology innovation, mechanism of action, real-world applications, future prospects, and overall impact on global healthcare.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand Moderna's biotechnology innovation.
- Study the working mechanism of mRNA technology.
- Analyze real-world healthcare applications.
- Explore future opportunities and challenges.
- Develop scientific presentation and research skills.

---

## 📑 Presentation Contents

1. Introduction
2. Biotechnology Innovation
3. Company Overview
4. mRNA Technology
5. How the Technology Works
6. Applications & Real-world Impact
7. Key Learnings & Takeaways
8. Conclusion & Future Outlook
9. References

---

## 🧬 Technologies Covered

- Messenger RNA (mRNA)
- Lipid Nanoparticles (LNP)
- Vaccine Development
- Precision Medicine
- Cancer Immunotherapy
- Personalized Healthcare
